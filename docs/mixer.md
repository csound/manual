# **Mixer Opcodes**

The Mixer family of opcodes provides a global mixer for Csound. The Mixer opcodes include [mixersend](opcodes/mixersend.md) for sending (that is, mixing in) an arate signal from any instrument to a channel of a mixer buss, [mixerreceive](opcodes/mixerreceive.md) for receiving an arate signal from a channel of any mixer buss in any instrument, [mixersetlevel](opcodes/mixersetlevel.md) (krate) and 
[mixersetleveli](opcodes/mixersetlevel_i.md) (irate) for controlling the level of the signal sent from a particular send to a particular buss, [mixergetlevel](opcodes/mixergetlevel.md) for reading (at krate) the level for sending a signal from a particular send to a particular buss, and [mixerclear](opcodes/mixerclear.md) for resetting the busses to zero before the next kperiod of a performance.
